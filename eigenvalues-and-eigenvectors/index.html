Eigenvalues and eigenvectors

The directions a matrix does not turn: how to find them, A = XΛX⁻¹ and powers, stability and steady states, symmetric matrices and positive definite bowls. 7 short slides, then worked examples and problems. Eigenvectors and Eigenvalues Explained Visually: Ax = λx

An eigenvector is a direction that a matrix only stretches, never turns: Ax = λx. Find one by dragging an arrow, with examples and Python. Strang §6.1.

🔒 MembersLesson 7det(A − λI) = 0Finding eigenvalues: make A − λI singular.Open slide →02 · Strang §6.1 · The characteristic equation

How to Find Eigenvalues: det(A − λI) = 0

Find eigenvalues by solving det(A − λI) = 0, then eigenvectors from the null space of A − λI. Trace and determinant checks, examples and Python. Strang §6.1.

🔒 MembersLesson 7A = XΛX⁻¹Diagonalizing: A in its own best coordinates.Open slide →03 · Strang §6.2 · Diagonalizing a matrix

Diagonalizing a Matrix: A = XΛX⁻¹ and Matrix Powers

With enough eigenvectors, A = XΛX⁻¹: eigenvectors in X, eigenvalues on the diagonal of Λ. Then powers are easy, Aᵏ = XΛᵏX⁻¹. Examples and Python. Strang §6.2.

🔒 MembersLesson 7StabilityRepeat A many times: the eigenvalues decide what survives.Open slide →04 · Strang §6.2 · Powers, stability and Markov matrices

Eigenvalues, Stability and Markov Matrices (Steady State)

Repeating A makes parts grow or decay by their eigenvalues. Markov matrices settle to a steady state, the eigenvector for λ = 1. Python examples. Strang §6.2.

🔒 MembersLesson 7Symmetric matricesSymmetric matrices: perpendicular eigenvectors.Open slide →05 · Strang §6.4 · Symmetric matrices

Symmetric Matrices: Real Eigenvalues and Orthogonal Eigenvectors

A symmetric matrix has real eigenvalues and perpendicular eigenvectors, so A = QΛQᵀ with Q orthogonal: the spectral theorem. Examples and Python. Strang §6.4.

🔒 MembersLesson 7Positive definitePositive definite: a bowl, not a saddle.Open slide →06 · Strang §6.5 · Positive definite matrices

Positive Definite Matrices: Bowls, Saddles and Eigenvalues

A symmetric matrix is positive definite when all its eigenvalues are positive, or xᵀAx > 0 for x ≠ 0: a bowl, not a saddle. Python examples. Strang §6.5.
