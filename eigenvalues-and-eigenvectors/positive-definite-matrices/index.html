Strang §6.5 · Positive definite matrices

Positive definite: a bowl, not a saddle.

Among symmetric matrices, the most important are the positive definite ones. All their eigenvalues are positive. Equivalently, the number xᵀAx is positive for every nonzero x. Plot the height z = xᵀAx above each point x of the plane and you get a bowl, with exactly one lowest point.

Lesson 7 · slide 6Positive definite
A positive definite ⇔ all λ > 0 ⇔ xᵀAx > 0 for all x ≠ 0
Key ideas
  1. A symmetric A is positive definite when all its eigenvalues are positive.
  2. Equivalent test: the energy xᵀAx > 0 for every nonzero x.
  3. For 2 by 2 [a b; b c]: a > 0 and ac − b² > 0. (Also: all pivots positive.)
  4. The graph of xᵀAx is a bowl. One negative eigenvalue turns it into a saddle.

A bowl or a saddle

Drop a marble into a salad bowl and it rolls to the single lowest point, from any side. Drop it on a horse saddle (or a Pringles chip) and it rolls off: the surface goes up in one direction and down in another. Positive definite matrices are the bowls.

The number xᵀAx

For A = [a b; b c] and x = (x₁, x₂), the number xᵀAx is a curved surface:

xᵀAx = a x₁² + 2b x₁x₂ + c x₂²
A = 2112:   2x₁² + 2x₁x₂ + 2x₂² = x₁² + x₂² + (x₁ + x₂)²
A sum of squares: positive unless x = 0. A bowl

The eigenvalue test

Write x in the perpendicular eigenvectors of A (slide 5): x = c₁q₁ + c₂q₂. Then xᵀAx = λ₁c₁² + λ₂c₂², because Aqᵢ = λᵢqᵢ and q₁ᵀq₂ = 0 (the cross terms vanish). If both λ are positive, this is positive for every nonzero x. If one λ is negative, moving along that eigenvector makes xᵀAx negative: a saddle.

A = 2112: λ = 3 and 1, both positive. Bowl
A = 2332: λ = 5 and −1. Saddle: x = (1, −1) gives xᵀAx = 2 − 6 + 2 = −2
