How it works

Every slide explains one idea with an everyday picture first: a boat with two thrusters, a shopping basket, a mirror. Then comes the maths, a worked example with real numbers, and where the idea shows up in drones, IMUs and robot arms.

Next to the text is a lab. The Python runs in your browser, nothing to install. Functions like show(A), combo(A, x, b), nullpic(A, x) and four(A) draw on the plane, and table(...) lays out matrices with their pivots. Each slide ends with a small exercise that checks your answer.
