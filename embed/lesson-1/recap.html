What you can now do.

Six ideas, all built on one operation: combining columns.

Key ideas
  1. Linear combinations cv + dw are the basic operation.
  2. Ax is a combination of the columns of A.
  3. The column space C(A) holds every Ax, and Ax = b is solvable when b is in it.
  4. A system can be read by rows (lines crossing) or by columns (columns combining to b). Both give the same answer.
  5. A matrix moves the plane. Its columns are where 10 and 01 land, and |det A| is the area factor.
  6. AB applies B first, then A. Usually AB ≠ BA.
  • §1.1 Linear combinations. Scale and add: steps of v plus steps of w.
  • §1.3 Ax by columns. The basket: each column is a food, x says how many of each, Ax is the total. Compute by rows, think by columns.
  • §1.3, §3.1 Column space. Every spot your steps can reach. Ax = b is solvable exactly when b is in C(A).
  • §2.1 Row and column pictures. Two lines crossing, or a recipe that makes b. Same answer.
  • §8.1, §5.3 Transformations. Graph paper pinned at the origin. The columns are where 10 and 01 land, and |det A| is the area factor.
  • §2.4 Products. Socks, then shoes. AB applies B first.

Next lesson: elimination (§2.2, §2.3, §2.6)

Solve Ax = b the way a computer does. Subtract multiples of one row from another until the matrix is a triangle. Then solve from the bottom up. Write those steps as matrices and you get A = LU.

Read along

Examples

Example 1

A = 1326. Describe C(A). Is 24 in it?

Show solutionHide solution

Column 2 = 3·column 1, so C(A) is the line through 12. 24 = 2·12 is on it, for example x = 20.

Example 2

Build the matrix that turns the plane by 90° and doubles every length. What is its determinant?

Show solutionHide solution

Start from R = 0−110, the 90° turn. Take 2R (multiply every entry by 2): 2R = 0−220. Lengths double, so areas grow 4 times: det = 0 − (−4) = 4.

Example 3

Solve 2x + y = 3, x + y = 2 any way you like (subtracting one equation from the other works), then check with the column picture.

Show solutionHide solution

Subtract: x = 1, then y = 1. Columns: 1·21 + 1·11 = 32 ✓
