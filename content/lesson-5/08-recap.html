  • §4.1 Orthogonal subspaces. North and east: dot product zero. Ax = 0 makes x perpendicular to every row, and the two pieces fill the input space.
  • §4.2 Projection onto a line. The shortest way to the road: p = x̂a with x̂ = aᵀb / aᵀa.

  • §4.3 Least squares. Three stopwatches: when Ax = b has no solution, solve AᵀAx̂ = Aᵀb. The average is the simplest case.

Find the best line C + Dt through (0, 0), (1, 1), (2, 1), (3, 2).

m = 4, Σt = 6, Σt² = 14, Σb = 4, Σtb = 0 + 1 + 2 + 6 = 9.

4C + 6D = 4 and 6C + 14D = 9. Then D = 0.6 and C = 0.1. Best line: 0.1 + 0.6t.
