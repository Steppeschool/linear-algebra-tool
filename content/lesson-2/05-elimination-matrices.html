Now we write each step as a matrix, so a computer can store the steps and reuse them. Think of an instruction card: “take 2 times row 1 away from row 2, leave the other rows alone”. An elimination matrix is that card written in numbers. It starts from the identity matrix I, with 1s on the diagonal and 0s elsewhere, which changes nothing: IA = A. Put −ℓ in row 2, column 1, and you get E₂₁. Slide 6 explains how to multiply two matrices; for now, E₂₁A simply applies E₂₁'s row step to A. E₂₁ = 100−ℓ10001    E₂₁A = A with row 2 − ℓ·(row 1)
  1. An elimination matrix E is the identity with one extra entry, −ℓ, below the diagonal.
  2. Multiplying EA performs that row operation on A.
  3. A row of EA is a combination of the rows of A.
  4. To undo E, change −ℓ to +ℓ. This is the key to A = LU on slide 7.

Why write a step as a matrix?

As matrices, the whole list of steps becomes one equation that a computer can store and reuse.

An instruction card in numbers

E₂₁ is the card "subtract 2 times row 1 from row 2, leave the rest alone", written as numbers. Multiplying by it follows the card.

E₂₁ = 100−210001

Why it works: rows combine rows

Read EA by rows: row i of EA is row i of E times A, a combination of the rows of A.

  • Row 1 of E₂₁ is 100: keep row 1.
  • Row 2 is −210: row 2 minus 2 times row 1.
  • Row 3 is 001: keep row 3.

A row times a matrix uses the row's numbers as weights on the matrix's rows. Row 2, with A from slide 3:

−210 A = −2·211 + 1·4−60 + 0·−272 = 0−8−2

That is the new row 2 from slide 3.

The whole process as one product

E₃₂ E₃₁ E₂₁ A = U

Read it right to left: E₂₁ is closest to A, so it acts first. Then E₃₁, then E₃₂.

Undoing a step

To undo E₂₁, add 2 times row 1 back. That matrix is E₂₁⁻¹ ("E₂₁ inverse"; lesson 3 covers inverses): the identity with +2 in the same spot. Every elimination matrix is undone by flipping the sign of its extra entry.

Next: We have been multiplying matrices on trust. Slide 6 shows exactly how a matrix times a matrix works. Then slide 7 uses the undo steps to factor A = LU.

E₂₁ = 100−210001 subtracts 2·row 1 from row 2.

E₂₁⁻¹ = 100210001 adds it back
E₂₁⁻¹E₂₁ = I

Only the sign of the multiplier changes.
