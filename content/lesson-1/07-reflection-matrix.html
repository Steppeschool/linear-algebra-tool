It swaps left and right. The F should keep its size and just face the other way. (x, y)  →  (−x, y)
  1. To build a matrix, decide where (1, 0) and (0, 1) land and use them as columns (slide 5).
  2. A mirror keeps lengths and areas, so |det A| = 1.
  3. A mirror flips the plane over, and the determinant shows that with a minus sign: det A = −1.
  4. Applying the same mirror twice brings everything back: A·A = I. Here I = 1001 is the identity matrix, which leaves every vector where it is.

A mirror you have seen

AMBULANCE is printed backwards on the front of the vehicle, so drivers read it the right way round in their mirror. A mirror keeps every size and distance, but swaps left and right.

How to build it

Use the rule from slide 5: where should the two landmarks go?

  • The mirror sends points on the right over to the left. So (1, 0) goes to (−1, 0).
  • Points on the y-axis stay put. So (0, 1) stays at (0, 1).

Those are the columns. Write them in, run the code, and watch the F.

What the determinant tells us

The F keeps its size, so |det A| must be 1. But it now reads backwards, like a sheet of paper turned over. No rotation can do that. The determinant marks the flip with a minus sign: det A = −1. Check the readout under the picture.

After you solve it

  • Mirror twice. Two mirrors show your face the right way round. Check in code that A @ A gives the identity matrix.
  • Another mirror. Try the mirror across the line y = x. Where do (1, 0) and (0, 1) go now?

Example 1 · Mirror across the line y = x

(1, 0) → (0, 1) and (0, 1) → (1, 0).

M = 0110    det M = −1
(2, 1) → (1, 2): the coordinates swap

Example 2 · Two mirrors make a turn

Mirror across the x-axis first, then across the y-axis (the right-hand matrix acts first):

−1001 100−1 = −100−1
det = (−1)·(−1) = 1

Two flips cancel. The result is the half turn: no mirror image left.

Example 3 · Rotation or mirror?

Is 01−10 a rotation or a mirror?

det = 0·0 − 1·(−1) = 1
(1, 0) → (0, −1) and (0, 1) → (1, 0)

The determinant is +1, so nothing is flipped. It is a rotation: a quarter turn clockwise.
