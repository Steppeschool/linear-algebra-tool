  • §2.5 The inverse. Rewind: A⁻¹ undoes A. It exists exactly when A has n pivots and det A ≠ 0.
  • §2.5 The 2 by 2 formula. Swap the diagonal, flip the other signs, divide by ad − bc.
  • §2.5 Products. Shoes off before socks: (AB)⁻¹ = B⁻¹A⁻¹.
  • §2.5 Gauss-Jordan. Carbon paper: eliminate on [A | I] until it reads [I | A⁻¹].
  • §2.7 Transposes. Turn the spreadsheet. Symmetric matrices are distance tables, and AᵀA is always one.
  • §2.7 Permutations. A new playlist order. P⁻¹ = Pᵀ, and row exchanges give PA = LU.
