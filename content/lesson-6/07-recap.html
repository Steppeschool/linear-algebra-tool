  1. det A is the area or volume factor; the sign says whether space is flipped.
  2. Three rules define it; elimination does not change it.
  3. Compute it from the pivots; cofactors give the explicit formula.
  4. Cramer's rule, A⁻¹ = Cᵀ/det A, areas, volumes and cross products all come from it.
  • §5.1 What it measures. Pizza dough: every area is multiplied by the same factor det A. Negative means flipped, zero means squashed.
  • §5.1 The three rules. The rules of a game: det I = 1, a swap flips the sign, linear in each row. Elimination steps are shears that keep det.
  • §5.1 Pivots. Squaring up a box: det A = ± the product of the pivots. det AB = det A det B, det Aᵀ = det A.
  • §5.2 Cofactors. The itemised receipt: expand along a row with signs + − +.
  • §5.3 Cramer and the inverse. A recipe card per unknown: xⱼ = det Bⱼ / det A, and A⁻¹ = Cᵀ / det A.
  • §5.3 Areas and cross products. A field from GPS corners: ½|det| of the edges. Torque τ = r × F.

Find det 210121012 by elimination.

Show solutionHide solution

Row 2 − ½·row 1 = (0, 3/2, 1). Row 3 − ⅔·row 2 = (0, 0, 4/3). Pivots 2, 3/2, 4/3, so det = 2 · 3/2 · 4/3 = 4.

2

A is 3 by 3 with det A = −2. Find det(3A), det(A⁻¹) and det(AᵀA).

Show solutionHide solution

det(3A) = 27 · (−2) = −54.   det(A⁻¹) = −½.   det(AᵀA) = (−2)(−2) = 4.
