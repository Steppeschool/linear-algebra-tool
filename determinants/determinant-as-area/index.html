Strang §5.1 · What the determinant measures

The determinant: how much A stretches area.

Every square matrix has one number attached to it, the determinant. It answers a geometric question: by what factor does the matrix stretch areas (in 2D) or volumes (in 3D)? A negative sign means space is also flipped, and zero means it is squashed flat.

Lesson 6 · slide 1Determinant
det A = ad − bc for A = abcd    area of image = |det A| × area
Key ideas
  1. det A is the factor by which A multiplies every area (2D) or volume (3D).
  2. det A < 0: the matrix also flips space, like a mirror.
  3. det A = 0: space is squashed into a line or a plane. Then A has no inverse.
  4. For 2 by 2, det A = ad − bc. Larger matrices follow from three rules (slide 2).

Rolling out pizza dough

Roll a ball of dough into a sheet and every topping on it grows by the same factor. A slice of pepperoni and an olive both get twice as big if the sheet doubles in area. It does not matter what shape the topping has.

A matrix acts on the plane the same way. It may turn and slant shapes, but it multiplies every area by one fixed number. That number is the determinant, written det A.

Reading it off the unit square

The easiest shape to follow is the unit square, with corners at 0, (1, 0), (0, 1) and (1, 1). Lesson 1 showed that A sends (1, 0) to its first column and (0, 1) to its second column. So the square becomes the parallelogram spanned by the two columns, and its area is |det A|.

A = 3002: the square becomes a 3 by 2 rectangle, area 6,   det A = 3·2 − 0·0 = 6 ✓
A = 2111: a slanted parallelogram,   det A = 2·1 − 1·1 = 1. Same area as the square.

The sign: was the plane flipped?

Turning from (1, 0) to (0, 1) the short way is a counterclockwise turn. Now turn from column 1 of A to column 2 the short way. If that turn is still counterclockwise, det A > 0. If it has become clockwise, the plane has been mirrored and det A < 0. The mirror from lesson 1, −1001, has det = −1: same area, flipped.

Zero: squashed flat

If the two columns point along the same line, the parallelogram is flat and its area is 0. Then many inputs land on the same output, so nothing can undo A. That is the most useful fact about determinants: A is invertible exactly when det A ≠ 0.
