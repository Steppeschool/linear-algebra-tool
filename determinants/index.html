Determinants

The number that says how much a matrix stretches area and volume: its rules, how to compute it, Cramer's rule, the inverse formula, and areas, volumes and cross products. 7 short slides, then worked examples and problems. Each slide has an everyday picture, a worked example and Python you can run right on the page.

Determinant Meaning: Area and Volume Scale Factor

The determinant says how much a matrix stretches area (2D) or volume (3D); its sign says whether space is flipped. Examples and Python. Strang §5.1.

Lesson 6Three rulesThree rules, and everything follows.Open slide →02 · Strang §5.1 · The properties of the determinant

Properties of Determinants: The Three Rules

Three rules define the determinant: det I = 1, a row swap flips the sign, det is linear in each row. All else follows. Examples and Python. Strang §5.1.

Lesson 6Det from pivotsEliminate, then multiply the pivots.Open slide →03 · Strang §5.1 · Determinants by elimination

Determinant by Elimination: Product of the Pivots

Compute any determinant by elimination: det A = ± (product of the pivots). Also det AB = det A det B and det Aᵀ = det A. Examples and Python. Strang §5.1.

Lesson 6CofactorsCofactors: a determinant from smaller ones.Open slide →04 · Strang §5.2 · Permutations and cofactors

Cofactor Expansion and the 3×3 Determinant Formula

The big formula and cofactor expansion: a 3×3 determinant from 2×2 pieces with signs + − +. Worked examples and Python. Strang §5.2.

Lesson 6Cramer's ruleCramer's rule: each unknown is a ratio of two determinants.Open slide →05 · Strang §5.3 · Cramer's rule and inverses

Cramer's Rule and the Inverse Matrix Formula

Cramer's rule solves Ax = b with ratios of determinants, and cofactors give A⁻¹ = Cᵀ / det A. When to use them and when not. Examples and Python. Strang §5.3.

Area of a Triangle, Volume and the Cross Product

Determinants give a triangle's area from its corners, a box's volume from its edges, and the cross product u × v. Robotics examples and Python. Strang §5.3.
