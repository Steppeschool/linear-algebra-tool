Strang §5.2 · Permutations and cofactors

Cofactors: a determinant from smaller ones.

There is also an explicit formula. Expand along one row: each entry times the determinant of what is left when you cross out its row and column, with signs alternating + − +. Good for 3 by 3 by hand, hopeless for big matrices.

Lesson 6 · slide 4Cofactors
det A = a₁₁C₁₁ + a₁₂C₁₂ + a₁₃C₁₃    C₁ⱼ = (−1)^(1+j) · det(A without row 1 and column j)
Key ideas
  1. The minor of entry aᵢⱼ is the determinant of A with row i and column j crossed out.
  2. The cofactor Cᵢⱼ is that minor times (−1)^(i+j): signs + − + in row 1, − + − in row 2, and so on, like a checkerboard.
  3. Expanding along any row (or column): det A = sum of entries × their cofactors.
  4. Fully expanded, a 3 by 3 determinant is a sum of 3! = 6 products, each using one entry from every row and every column; an n by n has n! of them.

An itemised receipt

Elimination gives the total quickly. Sometimes you want the itemised receipt instead: where each part of the total comes from. That is the cofactor formula. It writes det A as a sum of products, each product using exactly one entry from every row and every column.

The 2 by 2 case, seen as choices

For abcd there are two ways to pick one entry per row and per column: a with d (columns in the order 1, 2), or b with c (columns in the order 2, 1). The second order is one swap away from the first, so by rule 2 it gets a minus sign: det = ad − bc.

Expanding a 3 by 3 along row 1

Notation: aᵢⱼ is the entry in row i, column j of A.

Take each entry of row 1. Cross out its row and column. The 2 by 2 that remains is its minor. Multiply entry × minor, and attach the signs + − + across the row:

det A = a₁₁(a₂₂a₃₃ − a₂₃a₃₂) − a₁₂(a₂₁a₃₃ − a₂₃a₃₁) + a₁₃(a₂₁a₃₂ − a₂₂a₃₁)

Each bracket, with its sign, is a cofactor. The signs follow a checkerboard, starting with + in the top left corner: (−1)^(i+j) for row i, column j.

A worked example

K = 2114−60−272 (the matrix from lesson 2)
2·((−6)·2 − 0·7) − 1·(4·2 − 0·(−2)) + 1·(4·7 − (−6)·(−2))
= 2·(−12) − 1·8 + 1·16 = −24 − 8 + 16 = −16 ✓ (the same as the pivots 2 · (−8) · 1)
