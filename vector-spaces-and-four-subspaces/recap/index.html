Recap · Reading

What you can now do.

For any matrix, you can now say which equations Ax = b are solvable, how many solutions they have, and what those solutions look like.

Lesson 4 · slide 7Recap
dim C(Aᵀ) + dim N(A) = n    dim C(A) + dim N(Aᵀ) = m
Key ideas
  1. Subspaces are closed under adding and scaling.
  2. N(A) holds the solutions of Ax = 0, found from the special solutions.
  3. Every solution of Ax = b is x_p + x_n.
  4. Four subspaces with dimensions r, n − r, r, m − r.
