Columns first

By the end of this lesson you can
  • Read Ax as a mix of the columns of A, the idea everything else builds on
  • Predict where a matrix sends any point of the plane
  • Combine two moves into one matrix, and know why the order matters

Linear combinations, Ax as a mix of columns, the column space, row and column pictures, matrices that move the plane, and why AB is not BA. 8 short slides, then worked examples and problems. Each slide has an everyday picture, a worked example and Python you can run right on the page.
