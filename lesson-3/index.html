Inverse of a Product: Why (AB)⁻¹ = B⁻¹A⁻¹

To undo AB you undo A first, then B, so the order flips: (AB)⁻¹ = B⁻¹A⁻¹. Socks and shoes, a worked 2×2 example and live Python. Strang §2.5.

Gauss-Jordan: Finding the Inverse by Elimination

Put A next to the identity and eliminate until A becomes I. The identity turns into the inverse. Worked 3×3 example with rref in Python. Strang §2.5.

Transpose and Symmetric Matrices Explained

The transpose swaps rows and columns. Why (AB)ᵀ = BᵀAᵀ, what a symmetric matrix is, and why AᵀA is always symmetric. Python example. Strang §2.7.

Permutation Matrices: Reordering Rows

A permutation matrix reorders the rows of A. Its inverse is its transpose, and it is how elimination handles row exchanges: PA = LU. Python example. Strang §2.7.
